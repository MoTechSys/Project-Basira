# Basira messages audit: `messages/{ar,en}.json` and pipeline notice combinations

**Assumptions.** `state.py`, `verify.py` and `schemas.py` were not attached. I assumed the following:
- I do not know when `state.decide` emits `arabic_text_at_ref`, `quran_wins`, `matched_other_wording` or `claimed_source_mismatch`.
- I do not know when it sets `attach_grade`.
- I assumed `Grade` has only the fields `pipeline.py:_match_for` sets: `text`, `takhrij`, `version`, `url`.
- Attachments have no line numbers, so locations are `file:function`.

## Findings

| # | key | location | problem | safe rewrite (ar / en) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `notice.grade_line` | `pipeline.py:_hadith_notices` | Emitted whenever any HadeethEnc match has a grade, at any status. On `partial_match`/`needs_review`, the grade sits beside the user's *different* wording, which implies a grade on the quote. With mixed OHD and HadeethEnc matches, it is unclear which record it belongs to. `{grade_source}` is not a `Grade` field, so it may be unfilled. | ar: «حكم موسوعة الأحاديث النبوية (الإصدار {version}) على الحديث رقم {id}، لا على نصك، وليس حكمًا من بصيرة: «{grade_text}». التخريج: {takhrij}.» / en: "HadeethEnc (version {version}) grade for hadith no. {id}, not for your text and not Basira's: "{grade_text}". Takhrij: {takhrij}." Emit only when that record's status is `found`. |
| 2 | `notice.hadeethenc_link_only` with `grade_line` | `_hadith_notices` and `_match_for` (link mode) | The body is empty, but the grade and takhrij are still shown. The user sees a grade for text they cannot compare. "This hadith is listed" asserts the quote *is* that hadith, even on `partial_match`/`needs_review`. | ar: «أقرب مدخل وجدناه في موسوعة الأحاديث النبوية رقمه {id}؛ لا نعرض نصه هنا، فقارِنه بنصك عبر الرابط قبل الاعتماد على أي معلومة عنه.» / en: "The closest entry we found in the Prophetic Hadiths Encyclopedia is no. {id}; its text is not shown here. Compare it with yours via the link before relying on anything about it." |
| 3 | `status.found`, `status.partial_match`, `status.needs_review` | `_render` (`msg_key`) and `_match_for` (`source_text=""`) | These promise "source text is shown next to yours" / "closest text… shown below". That is false when the only winner is a link-only HadeethEnc record. | Add a separate key used when all shown matches have an empty body. ar: «وُجد في {source_name}، {ref}. نص المصدر غير معروض هنا؛ افتح الرابط لقراءته.» / en: "Found in {source_name}, {ref}. The source text is not shown here; open the link to read it." |
| 4 | `notice.arabic_text_at_ref` | emitted via `d.notice_keys`; `_render` | It promises text after the colon. Nothing checks that a non-empty body follows. With `not_found_quran` it would either show nothing or leak a candidate, breaking the rule that Quran `not_found` shows no candidates. In link mode the body is empty. | ar: «النص الموجود في مصادرنا تحت الموضع المذكور في نصك ({ref}) معروض أدناه للمقارنة، ولا يعني ذلك أنه نصك.» / en: "The text in our sources at the location you cited ({ref}) is shown below for comparison; this does not mean it is your text." Emit only when a match at `{ref}` has non-empty `source_text`. Never emit it with Quran `not_found`. |
| 5 | `notice.claimed_source_mismatch` | `_render` | "not in {claimed}" is absolute, but we only checked our copy and numbering. It implies the user's attribution is false. | ar: «وجدنا النص في {found_in}، ولم نجده في نسختنا من {claimed} المذكور في نصك؛ قد يكون فيه بطبعة أو رواية لا تغطيها مصادرنا.» / en: "We found the text in {found_in}; we did not find it in our copy of {claimed}, cited in your text. It may appear there in an edition or transmission our sources do not cover." |
| 6 | `notice.other_nine_referral` with `no_grade` | `_hadith_notices` | Scholar referral fires only for non-Sahihain books; Sahihain matches get only `no_grade`. The contrast implies Sahihain hadiths need no scrutiny and the others do, which is a tier-based soft grading. | Make it tier-neutral and emit it for every hadith match. ar: «لكلام أهل العلم في أي حديث راجِع الموسوعة الحديثية في الدرر السنية.» / en: "For scholars' discussion of any hadith, see the Hadith Encyclopedia at Dorar.net." |
| 7 | `notice.hadith_variant` | `state` notice keys | It offers only the exculpatory explanation (another transmission), nudging toward "your wording is valid". The Quran counterpart is balanced; this one is not. | ar: «الأحاديث تُروى بألفاظ متقاربة في كتب مختلفة؛ الاختلاف المظلّل قد يكون سهوًا في النقل أو لفظًا في رواية لا تغطيها مصادرنا، ولا نحكم بأيٍّ منهما.» / en: "Hadiths are transmitted with close wordings across books; the highlighted difference may be a copying slip or a wording in a transmission our sources do not cover. We do not decide which." |
| 8 | `status.not_found_quran` | `state.py` message key | «قد يكون النص ليس آية» / "may not be a verse" leans toward a verdict. It also lacks the "not a judgment" disclaimer that hadith `not_found` has. | ar: «لم نجد هذا النص بين آيات المصحف في مصادرنا (رواية حفص عن عاصم). هذا ليس حكمًا عليه؛ قد يختلف عن نص مصادرنا اختلافًا لا يغطيه الفحص الآلي. راجِع مصحفًا معتمدًا.» / en: "We did not find this text among the Quran verses in our sources (Hafs from ʿAsim). This is not a judgment on it; it may differ from our sources in a way automatic checking does not cover. Please check an approved Mushaf." |
| 9 | `notice.no_grade` | `_hadith_notices` (else branch) | "this hadith" asserts the quote is a hadith, and hints a grade exists but is being withheld. | ar: «لا نعرض أحكامًا؛ نعرض موضع النص في المصادر فقط.» / en: "We do not show grades; we show where the text appears in the sources only." |
| 10 | `notice.quran_wins` | `state` notice keys | "matches a verse" overclaims if the key is ever paired with a fuzzy or `needs_review` Quran result. | ar: «وُجد النص في المصحف أيضًا؛ نعرض موضعه أولًا.» / en: "The text was also found in the Quran; its Mushaf location is shown first." Gate on Quran `found`. |
| 11 | `notice.ohd_display_note` | `_match_for` (`rec.display`) | "direction marks removed" admits the displayed text differs from the corpus bytes. This is in tension with constraint 1 (byte-exact display). | One-line disagreement: stripping should happen at build time into a hashed `display` field that `verify.py` compares byte-exact. Then: ar «نص الحديث كما في حقل العرض من مجموعة Open-Hadith-Data.» / en "Hadith text as in the display field of the Open-Hadith-Data collection." |
| 12 | `status.found_multi` with `notice.many_positions` | `_render` | Both fire when `total > shown`, which duplicates the message. `{source_name}` is singular when winners span corpora. Arabic «{count} موضعًا» is wrong for 3–10. These are not safety issues. | Drop `many_positions` when `msg_key == found_multi`. ar: «وُجد في {count} {مواضع/موضعًا}…» (plural chosen in code) / en unchanged. |
| 13 | `$comment` | `ar.json`, `en.json` | Contains the banned words «محرّف»، «صحيح/ضعيف», "fabricated", "authentic/weak". If the file is ever served to the client, they reach the UI. | Strip `$comment` in `load_messages` or at the API boundary. |
